Your account, your data

A few things worth knowing before you sign in.

  • Lareon only opens lareon.org pages inside the app. Other links open in your browser.
  • Your password goes to Lareon directly. This app never sees or stores it.
  • You stay signed in on this computer until you choose Sign out.
